# FreePDF 7.6.0

FreePDF 7.6.0 signs a form's existing, empty signature field, such as a "sign
here" field, instead of always adding a new field next to it.

## New

- **Signing an existing signature field.** Name the field in
  `DigitalSignatureOptions.FieldName`:

  ```csharp
  using var document = PdfReader.Open("form.pdf", PdfDocumentOpenMode.Append);
  DigitalSignatureHandler.ForDocument(document, signer,
      new DigitalSignatureOptions { FieldName = "Approval", Reason = "Approved" });
  document.Save("signed.pdf");
  ```

  - The signature becomes the field's value and is drawn in its widget at the
    widget's size; leave `Rectangle` and `PageIndex` unset. A field without area
    gives an invisible signature.
  - The field keeps its name and flags. The page and the form's field list stay
    as they are: the form gets no new field.
  - A field lock (`/Lock`) is recorded in the signature as a FieldMDP reference,
    as Acrobat and pyHanko record it, so validators lock the fields it names from
    then on. A lock with a document permission (`/P`) passes it on.
  - Required seed values (`/SV`) are checked. A required reason must be given as
    `Reason`; where the field allows none (no reasons or a single "." listed), no
    reason may be given and the signature has none. Requirements FreePDF cannot
    meet or check throw `NotSupportedException`: a signing certificate, a digest
    method, a timestamp, a certification, revocation information, locking the
    document or a named appearance. Optional seed values are ignored.
  - Mistakes throw when the handler is created: no field of that name, not a
    signature field, a field with fields below it, a field that is already
    signed, a lock without a valid `/Action`, or unmet seed values.
  - In `Append` mode the signature rules of 7.5 apply: a certification with
    "form filling and signing" (P=2) or more allows signing an existing field,
    "no changes" (P=1) refuses it, and a field that an earlier signature locks is
    refused when saving. It works in `Modify` mode too.

## Upgrading from 7.5.x

Update all FreePDF packages to 7.6.0 together and rebuild; the assembly version
is now 7.6.0.0. The public API gains `DigitalSignatureOptions.FieldName`; nothing
else changes, and signing without `FieldName` adds a new field as before.

pyHanko's default policy accepts signing an existing field when the field is its
own widget, as pyHanko and most tools make them. When the field's widget is a
separate object, or it has several widgets or shares its appearance with another
widget, pyHanko reports the document's earlier signatures as changed; pyHanko's
own signer fails the same way on a shared appearance, refuses fields with several
widgets, and does not update a separate widget's appearance.

## Validation

All 7.5.2 release gates apply, plus:

- 35 regression tests: a field that is its own widget, one with a separate widget
  whose "sign here" appearance is replaced, an invisible field, swapped widget
  rectangles, `Modify` mode, the mistakes above, a lock recorded and then enforced
  on the next save, a lock with `/P`, each certification level, a field locked by
  an earlier signature, seed values including the reason conventions, and the
  custom appearance; 641 regression tests in all;
- pyHanko 0.29.1: forms that pyHanko made with an empty signature field, signed by
  FreePDF in `Append` mode with the field count unchanged. A plain form, a form
  with a lock on another field, forms with a required reason or with no reason
  allowed, and a form certified with P=2 are VALID (the certification as "signing
  and form filling", also in poppler's `pdfsig`); P=1 and a disallowed reason are
  refused. After pyHanko changed the locked field, it judged FreePDF's signature
  INVALID, while the same change without the lock stayed VALID;
- an independent review in two rounds, whose findings were fixed before release.
  It found locks, including `/P`, enforced by FreePDF and pyHanko, encrypted
  `Append` documents (AES-128 and AES-256) signed correctly, and the path that
  adds a new field unchanged.

See the migration guide's "Signed documents" section (`../migrating-to-7.md`)
and the support policy (`../support-policy.md`).
